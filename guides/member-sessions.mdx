# Sessions in a member's Saturday

When an athlete on your platform is linked to their Saturday account, the sessions you plan for them can appear in their Saturday app with a fuel plan for each, and follow your changes. Nothing extra to call: the [activity routes](/guides/activities) you use today do it.

This needs two things:

1. **Saturday's go-ahead for your platform.** Ask at api@saturday.fit. Until then the routes below behave exactly as before and their answers carry no `member_session`.
2. **The member's yes.** When a member confirms the link to your platform (the confirmation page Saturday emails, or your checkout on saturday.fit), the page says "{your name} will add the sessions it plans for you to Saturday." Confirming is their yes. A member linked before that sentence was shown gives it by turning your sessions on in the Saturday app (Preferences, Partner Sharing), or by confirming again. The same switch turns your sessions off.

## Adding a session

Create the activity as usual, with a `start` and your own `external_id`:

```json
POST /v1/athletes/{athlete_id}/activities
{
  "type": "run",
  "duration_min": 60,
  "intensity_level": 5,
  "start": "2026-12-02T07:00:00-07:00",
  "external_id": "your-session-4411"
}
```

The answer carries what happened in the member's Saturday:

```json
{
  "id": "…",
  "type": "run",
  "start": "2026-12-02T07:00:00-07:00",
  "member_session": { "state": "added" }
}
```

The member's session is matched on `external_id`. Sending the same session again, or retrying a request, updates the one session the member has.

## Changing and moving a session

`PATCH` the activity. The member's session takes your change, and `member_session` reads `updated`. A field the member changed in Saturday stays as they set it, and `kept_fields` lists it:

```json
{ "member_session": { "state": "updated", "kept_fields": ["duration_min"] } }
```

To move a session, send a new `start`. A session the member moved stays where they put it.

## Removing a session

`DELETE` the activity. The session leaves the member's Saturday unless the member used it (changed it, planned fuel or bottles for it, reported on it, rated it, wrote a note or marked it done), or another of your activities for this athlete holds the same `external_id`. A session the member used stays theirs.

## When a session is not added

`member_session.state` is `not_added`, and `code` says why:

| `code` | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `not_linked` | The athlete is not linked to a Saturday account | Nothing; sessions start once they link |
| `no_member_yes` | The member linked before agreeing that you add their sessions | They agree by turning your sessions on in the Saturday app, or when they confirm the link again |
| `member_turned_off` | The member turned your sessions off in Saturday | Nothing; sessions already added stay with them |
| `start_required` | The activity has no `start` | Send `start` |
| `start_needs_offset` | Saturday holds no time zone for the member | Send `start` with a UTC offset |
| `legs_not_added` | Bricks and triathlons stay on your record only | Nothing |
| `member_deleted` | The member deleted this session in Saturday | Nothing; it stays deleted |
| `not_partner_session` | The member's Saturday holds a session there that you did not add | Nothing; it is left as it is |
| `try_again` | Saturday could not write the session | `PATCH` the activity with the same values |

The activity itself is stored on your record in every case, and its prescription works as before.
