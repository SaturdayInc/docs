When an athlete on your platform has connected a Saturday account, Saturday's calculations for that athlete use their Saturday app answers to fill anything you have not stored. Seeing those answers yourself is a separate step, and the athlete decides it: one choice per partner platform, off until they turn it on. This page is the narrative first, written so a developer or an AI assistant building your integration knows what to do in each state, then the reference.

The answers here are the athlete's Saturday app answers, held on their Saturday account. They are not the `settings` you store on your athlete record, which you read with `GET /v1/athletes/{athlete_id}/settings` (see [Athletes](/guides/athletes)).

## State machine

Every athlete on your platform is in exactly one sharing state. `GET /v1/athletes/{athlete_id}` carries it as `profile_sharing`, and `GET /v1/athletes/{athlete_id}/fueling-profile` carries it as `sharing`.

| State | What it means | What your code does |
|---|---|---|
| `not_linked` | No Saturday account is connected to this athlete record | Nothing to read. Calculations use what you store. |
| `off` | A Saturday account is connected, and the athlete has not chosen to share their answers with you | Do not call the route on a timer. Show what you store. Wait for `athlete.profile_sharing_changed`. |
| `on` | The athlete shares their answers with you | Read `GET /v1/athletes/{athlete_id}/fueling-profile`. Read it again on `athlete.fueling_profile_updated`. |

What moves an athlete between states, and the webhook each move sends:

| Move | Cause | Webhook |
|---|---|---|
| `not_linked` → `off` | The athlete confirms the connection to their Saturday account | None from sharing. `GET /v1/athletes/{athlete_id}` shows `off`. |
| `off` → `on` | The athlete turns sharing on in the Saturday app, or confirms the hosted page's email | `athlete.profile_sharing_changed`, record shows `profile_sharing: "on"` |
| `on` → `off` | The athlete turns sharing off in the app or on the hosted page, or their account now reads as under 18 | `athlete.profile_sharing_changed`, record shows `profile_sharing: "off"` |
| `on` → `not_linked` | The connection to the Saturday account goes | `athlete.profile_sharing_changed`, record shows `profile_sharing: "not_linked"` |
| `not_linked` → `on` | A connection arrives for a Saturday account that shares with you | `athlete.profile_sharing_changed`, record shows `profile_sharing: "on"` |
| `on`, answers change | The athlete changes a shared answer in the Saturday app | `athlete.fueling_profile_updated`, naming the fields |

An athlete's account connects to your athlete record through a Saturday checkout link they open from your platform, or through an exact email match that the athlete confirms from an email Saturday sends to their Saturday address. See [Freemium Model, Automatic linking by email](/guides/freemium-model#automatic-linking-by-email).

## What the athlete sees

- **In the Saturday app.** Preferences shows a Partner Sharing card once their Saturday account is connected to at least one partner platform, with one switch per connected platform: "Share my fueling profile with \{your platform\}". The line under the switch lists what you see when it is on and says their numbers on your platform stay the same either way. A switch takes effect at once, on or off.
- **On Saturday's hosted onboarding page.** The finish screen asks "Let \{your platform\} see your fueling answers?" when the athlete's Saturday account is connected to your platform, the athlete is 18 or older, and the account has a verified email address. Yes emails that address a one-tap confirmation, and sharing starts when the athlete taps it. No turns sharing off at once. An athlete who shares sees a Stop sharing button instead.
- **Under 18.** No switch in the app and no question on the hosted page. You see `off`, with the same message as any athlete who has not shared, so a response never reveals an age.
- **No expiry.** Sharing stays on until the athlete turns it off, the accounts are disconnected, or the Saturday account is deleted.

The hosted page's Yes needs the email step because you hold the hosted page's link and send it yourself, so a tap on that page alone cannot show that the athlete made the choice. The email goes to the athlete's Saturday address, which you do not control. Its link works for 7 days. Tapping Yes again sends a new link, at most one every 10 minutes and three in all, until the request expires.

## The journey, narrated

**1. You create the athlete with their email.**
They use the Saturday app under the same address. Saturday emails that address asking them to confirm the connection to your platform. Until they confirm, `profile_sharing` is `not_linked`.

*Your code:* nothing. Calculations use what you store.

**2. They confirm the connection.**
Their Saturday app answers start filling the gaps in what you store, at calculation time. `profile_sharing` reads `off`: they have not chosen to share the answers themselves.

*Your code:* if you want the answers, tell the athlete they can share them with you from the Saturday app or from Saturday's hosted onboarding page. Do not poll the route.

**3. They turn sharing on.**
In the app that is one switch. On the hosted page it is Yes and then the link in the email. Either way you receive `athlete.profile_sharing_changed`, carrying the athlete record with `profile_sharing: "on"`.

*Your code:* call `GET /v1/athletes/{athlete_id}/fueling-profile` and store what it returns.

**4. They change an answer in the app.**
You receive `athlete.fueling_profile_updated`: the athlete record plus `changed_fields`, the names of the answers that changed, never their values.

*Your code:* read the route again for the new values.

**5. They stop sharing, or disconnect.**
You receive `athlete.profile_sharing_changed` with `profile_sharing` at `off`, or at `not_linked` when the connection itself went. The route returns no answers from then on.

*Your code:* stop treating the answers you hold as the athlete's current Saturday answers. How long you keep what you read is your platform's call under your privacy policy.

## GET /v1/athletes/\{athlete_id\}/fueling-profile

Returns the athlete's sharing state, and their Saturday app answers when it is `on`. Call it with your partner API key, or with an athlete-delegated OAuth token for that same athlete.

<CodeGroup>

```python Python
import os
import requests

athlete_id = "a1b2c3d4-e5f6-4a7b-8c9d-0e1f2a3b4c5d"
response = requests.get(
    f"https://api.saturday.fit/v1/athletes/{athlete_id}/fueling-profile",
    headers={"Authorization": f"Bearer {os.environ['SATURDAY_API_KEY']}"},
)
response.raise_for_status()
body = response.json()

if body["sharing"] == "on":
    for field, answer in body["profile"].items():
        print(f"{field}: {answer['value']} (calculations use {answer['calculations_use']})")
else:
    print(f"{body['sharing']}: {body['message']}")
```

```typescript TypeScript
const athleteId = "a1b2c3d4-e5f6-4a7b-8c9d-0e1f2a3b4c5d";
const response = await fetch(
  `https://api.saturday.fit/v1/athletes/${athleteId}/fueling-profile`,
  { headers: { Authorization: `Bearer ${process.env.SATURDAY_API_KEY}` } }
);
if (!response.ok) throw new Error(`Saturday returned ${response.status}`);
const body = await response.json();

if (body.sharing === "on") {
  for (const [field, answer] of Object.entries(body.profile)) {
    console.log(`${field}: ${JSON.stringify(answer.value)} (calculations use ${answer.calculations_use})`);
  }
} else {
  console.log(`${body.sharing}: ${body.message}`);
}
```

</CodeGroup>

### Response fields

| Field | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `object` | string | Always `fueling_profile` |
| `athlete_id` | string | The athlete you asked about |
| `sharing` | string | `on`, `off`, or `not_linked` |
| `message` | string or null | `null` when `sharing` is `on`; otherwise the reason there are no answers, the two sentences above |
| `profile` | object | Only when `sharing` is `on`. One entry per shared answer, below |
| `updated_at` | integer | Only when `sharing` is `on`. When the athlete's Saturday account record was last saved, Unix milliseconds. Any save moves it, including one that changes no shared answer, so use `athlete.fueling_profile_updated` to learn that answers changed |

Each `profile` entry is an object with `value` and `calculations_use`. The names are the calculation-side names (`age` and `athlete_weight_kg`, as a calculate request takes them), and `concerns` holds the eight concern flags in one object:

| Entry | `value` |
|---|---|
| `sex` | `male`, `female`, or `intersex`, as the athlete answered |
| `age` | Whole years, the current year minus the athlete's birth year; `null` outside 10 to 100 |
| `athlete_weight_kg` | Kilograms to one decimal; `null` outside 20 to 250 kg |
| `sweat_level` | Integer 1 (light) to 9 (heavy) |
| `saltiness` | Integer 1 (not salty) to 9 (very salty) |
| `satiety_level` | Integer 1 (mostly whole food) to 9 (high-octane fuel) |
| `fitness_level` | Integer 1 (beginner) to 9 (elite) |
| `carb_experience` | A range token, as in `settings`: `range_0_30`, `range_40_60`, `range_gt_70` |
| `usual_carb_consumption` | A range token, as in `settings`: `range_lt_60`, `range_60_80`, `range_80_100`, `range_gt_100` |
| `concerns` | An object of eight booleans: `performance`, `gut_distress`, `heat_tolerance`, `muscle_cramps`, `faintness`, `hunger`, `thirst`, `drinking_resistance` |

`value` is `null` when the athlete has not answered that question in the app, or holds an answer outside what the app writes. `concerns` is `null` when they never answered the concerns question; an object of all `false` means they answered "none of these".

### calculations_use

`value` is always the app answer. `calculations_use` says where the value Saturday's calculations use for this athlete comes from, field by field:

| `calculations_use` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `partner` | Your athlete record holds a value for this field, and calculations use yours. An inline value on a calculate call still wins for that call |
| `saturday_app` | Your record has no value for this field, and calculations use the app answer |
| `default` | Neither has a value. Calculations fall back to a default, the field shows up in `precision.missing_fields`, and the numbers come back as ranges |

`partner` with a `value` that differs from what you store means the athlete answered differently in the two places. Calculations use yours; the app answer is there if you want to ask the athlete which is right.

### Errors

| Status | `type` and `code` | When |
|---|---|---|
| `401`, `403` | `authentication_error` | Missing, invalid, or revoked credentials. See [Authentication](/authentication) |
| `403` | `authorization_error`, `athlete_scope` | An athlete-delegated token naming a different athlete |
| `404` | `resource_not_found`, `athlete_not_found` | No athlete with this id under your partner account |
| `429` | `rate_limit_error` | See [Rate Limiting](/rate-limiting) |
| `500` | `api_error`, `storage_error` | Saturday could not read the athlete or their Saturday account; retry with backoff |

Errors use the standard envelope in [Error Handling](/error-handling). An athlete who shares nothing is never an error: that is `200` with `sharing` set to `off` or `not_linked`.

## profile_sharing on GET /v1/athletes/\{athlete_id\}

Reading one athlete returns `profile_sharing` beside `subscription_status` (the record below is trimmed to the fields that matter here):

```json
{
  "id": "a1b2c3d4-e5f6-4a7b-8c9d-0e1f2a3b4c5d",
  "external_id": "your-user-12345",
  "subscription_status": "full",
  "profile_sharing": "off",
  "profile_complete": true
}
```

The values are the same three as `sharing`. The field is computed on each read of one athlete. It is in the athlete record the two sharing webhooks carry, and not on `GET /v1/athletes` lists or in the record that `athlete.created`, `athlete.updated` and `athlete.profile_completed` carry. It is absent when Saturday could not read the state on that request; call the fueling-profile route for the answer.

## Webhooks

Two events cover sharing. Subscribe to them by name when you register or update a webhook; full payloads are in [Webhooks](/guides/webhooks#profile-sharing-event-payloads).

- `athlete.profile_sharing_changed`: `data` is the athlete record, as `GET /v1/athletes/{athlete_id}` returns it, with `profile_sharing` showing the new state.
- `athlete.fueling_profile_updated`: `data` is the same record plus `changed_fields`, the names of the shared answers that changed. Sent only while sharing stays on.

Like every athlete record, `data` names the athlete in `id`, not `athlete_id`.

A webhook is a cue to read the route, which is the source of truth. If one Saturday account is connected to more than one of your athlete records, each record gets an event.

## MCP

Partner MCP connections get a `get_fueling_profile` tool taking `athlete_id`, which returns this route's response. `get_athlete` carries `profile_sharing`, so an agent knows when to call it. See [MCP Integration](/guides/mcp-integration#tool-catalog).
