# Getting Access

There are four ways to build with Saturday. Which one fits depends on where your athletes' records are.

| Way in | Who | Athletes' records are in | Auth | Money |
|------|-----|-------------------|------|-------|
| Consumer app | An athlete | the Saturday app | app account | app subscription |
| **Coach portal** | A coach whose athletes use the Saturday app | **the Saturday app** | portal login (coach API keys are closed for now) | any paid coach plan |
| **Your own API key** | Developers, coaching businesses and teams building their own tools | **your system** | an `sk_` key you make at saturday.fit/api | comes with any paid Saturday plan |
| **Platform partner** | A platform embedding Saturday for its whole user base | **the partner's system** | `sk_` key via agreement | revenue share; athletes subscribe for exact numbers |

## Your own API key (comes with your Saturday plan)

You're building a product, a tool or your coaching setup, and you keep your athletes' records.

- **Get it:** sign in at [saturday.fit/api](https://saturday.fit/api) with your Saturday account and make your key. It comes with any paid Saturday plan, member or coach: a subscription from the App Store, Google Play or the web, Lifetime, or a paid coach plan. The free trial alone does not include one. The key is shown once and emailed to your sign-in address once it is verified.
- **Lost it?** Make a new one at saturday.fit/api. The old one stops working at once, and your athletes stay.
- **Athletes:** a key covers 10 athletes. To cover more, write to [api@saturday.fit](mailto:api@saturday.fit) about what you're building and we'll raise it.
- **Trial:** each athlete you add gets the standard [full-precision trial](/guides/freemium-model#full-precision-trial) (15 calls day one, then 5/day, up to 42 in all) automatically. A key starts that trial for 10 athletes over its life, so deleting an athlete frees no trial; when we raise your athletes, the trials rise with them. An athlete whose Saturday account has used its own free trial gets ranges until they subscribe.
- **Limits:** 2 requests/second and 200 calls/day per account. Write to [api@saturday.fit](mailto:api@saturday.fit) when you need more.
- **If your plan ends:** the key answers `403 plan_inactive` until the plan is active again, then works with your athletes as they were.

## Coach portal (your athletes use the Saturday app)

If you coach athletes who use the Saturday app, work in the [coach portal](https://coach.saturday.fit): your roster and alerts, and paying for your athletes' Saturday plans. Coach API keys (`cp_`) are closed for now; the [Coach API guide](/guides/coach-api) has the details. A paid coach plan also comes with your own API key at saturday.fit/api, for athletes whose records you keep.

## Platform partnership

You run a training platform and want Saturday inside it for all your users: partner-namespaced athletes, revenue share, [bundle and team offers](/guides/freemium-model#how-discounts-stack) and webhooks. Write to [api@saturday.fit](mailto:api@saturday.fit).

<Note>
  A large team with athletes in the Saturday app and tooling of its own fits a partnership. Write to [api@saturday.fit](mailto:api@saturday.fit).
</Note>

## Discovery for agents

`GET https://api.saturday.fit/v1/` (no auth) lists the endpoints at their current stage, how to get a key, and how the trial works. Site context: [saturday.fit/llms.txt](https://saturday.fit/llms.txt).
